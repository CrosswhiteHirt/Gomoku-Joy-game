Gomoku Joy Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Gomoku Joy is an offline Gomoku board game. This policy explains how the current version of the app handles information.

Information we collect

Gomoku Joy does not require an account or login and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

Local game data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device so that an unfinished game can be restored. This information is not uploaded to us and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and purchases

The current version does not display advertisements and does not process in-app purchases. If a future version adds third-party advertising or Google Play Billing, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.

Third-party services

The current version does not send personal information to third-party analytics, advertising, or authentication services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app is intended for general audiences, parents and guardians should still supervise children's use of mobile applications.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality changes. The latest version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact method shown on the Gomoku Joy Google Play store listing.
